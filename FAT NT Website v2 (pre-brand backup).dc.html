About FAT NT

Practical skills for people and workplaces

FAT NT provides training for learners and organisations in the Northern Territory.

Our story

Built in the Territory, for the Territory

FAT NT started with a simple idea: workplace training should be practical, local and easy to access. What began as a handful of first aid sessions has grown into a broad program covering safety, emergency response and wellbeing for workers right across the Northern Territory.

We work with individuals building their careers and with employers who need a dependable training partner — from small local businesses to large multi-site operations.

“Our goal is for every learner to walk out confident they can act when it matters.”

— Founder, FAT NT
