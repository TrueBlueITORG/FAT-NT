About FAT NT

Territory proud. Territory owned.

FAT NT is a Darwin-based first aid training company — proudly owned and operated by Territorians, for Territorians.

Our story

Built in the Territory, for the Territory

The NT now has its very own first aid training company. We believe first aid training should be practical, local and genuinely useful — this is not a tick-and-flick course. We teach practical, life-saving skills in a timely, business-friendly format.

We specialise in corporate and remote training, tailoring every course to the industry and prioritising the skills that matter most in your workplace. And tailoring your training does not cost extra.

“We do it because we love what we do — and we love to save lives.”

— Founder, FAT NT
