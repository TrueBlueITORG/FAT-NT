FAT NT provides training for learners and organisations across the Northern Territory.

Our story

“Our goal is for every learner to walk out confident they can act when it matters.”

— Founder, FAT NT

FAT NT started with a simple idea: workplace training should be practical, local and easy to access. What began as a handful of first aid sessions has grown into a broad program covering safety, emergency response and wellbeing for workers right across the Territory.

We work with individuals building their careers and with employers who need a dependable training partner — from small local businesses to large multi-site operations.
